## TL;DR

- Patanjali Foods Limited reported its Q2 and H1 FY27 results, recording operating revenue of INR11,323 crores for Q2 (up 16% YoY) and INR22,660 crores for H1. Growth was supported by strong performances in the edible oil and biscuits segments, despite headwinds from commodity inflation and rural demand pressures.

## Management Commentary

- Revenue from operations for Q2 stood at INR11,323 crores, up 16% year-on-year, with operating EBITDA of INR426 crores.
- First-half revenue from operations reached INR22,660 crores, up 22% year-on-year, with operating EBITDA of INR969 crores.
- Edible oil segment achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue of INR8,532 crores, up 22% year-on-year.
- FMCG quarterly revenue stood at INR2,923 crores with EBITDA of INR221 crores.
- The government reduced the effective import duty on crude palm oil and crude soybean oil from 16.5% to 11%, and on crude sunflower oil from 16.5% to 5.5% on September 24th, which the company is passing on to consumers.

## Guidance & Outlook

- Expect to close the year at a broad basis 15% growth in HPC.
- Expect commodity inflation to continue in the near term.

## Risks & Concerns

- Steady rise in inflation through the quarter (from 4.45% in July to 6.09% in October) and high food inflation at 8.94%.
- Monsoon ended with a 13% rainfall deficit, affecting Kharif harvest and Rabi sowing.
- Muted rural demand amid inflationary pressures.
- Rising commodity costs including wheat, sugar, milk procurement, soybean, crude-linked inputs, and logistics/freight costs.

## Analyst Q&A Highlights

**Q:** Ashutosh from ICICI Securities asked about competitive intensity in the toothpaste and biscuit categories and the performance of herbal markets.

**A:** Management stated that while competitive intensity is high in dental care due to new launches and pricing, Patanjali Dant Kanti grew 16% compared to Q1 (revenue of INR378 crores). In biscuits, growth is largely volume-led with distribution reach expanding to over 3 lakh direct outlets.

---

**Q:** Abhishek Mathur from Systematix Group inquired about volume versus realization trends in biscuits, skincare, and edible oils, and reasons for decline in staples/ethnic foods.

**A:** Management noted biscuits and skincare growth is primarily volume-led, while edible oil revenue growth is largely driven by unit price increases with about 3% volume growth. Staples saw a conscious decision to de-grow due to commodity inflation challenges to protect margins.

---

**Q:** Dhiraj Mistry from Jefferies asked about inventory losses following import duty reductions in September.

**A:** Management stated the impact was minimal because a large part of inventory was afloat or in bonded non-duty customs warehouses, and inventory is tracked daily with P&L adjustments accounted for within the same quarter.

---

**Q:** Priya Kulkarni from CN Capital asked about the financial impact of edible oil packaging standardization.

**A:** Management stated there is no negative financial impact as adequate time was given to run through existing packaging inventory.
