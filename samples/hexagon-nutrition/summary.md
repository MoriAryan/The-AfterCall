## Management Commentary

- Revenue from operations grew 27% year-on-year to INR214 crores for H1 FY27, with Q2 revenue up 15% year-on-year and 5% sequentially to INR110 crores.
- Gross margin improved sequentially by about 310 basis points from Q1 to 47% in Q2, driven by pricing, procurement, and product mix initiatives.
- Strategic inventory rose from INR84 crores in March to INR136 crores in September to secure key raw material supplies for confirmed H2 orders, and is expected to normalize by March 2027.
- Nashik facility reconstruction work has been initiated and is being tracked on a timely and regular basis.
- The balance sheet remains strong, with the company net cash positive by around INR9 crores, total equity at INR245 crores, and a debt-to-equity ratio of about 0.15.

## Guidance & Outlook

- Maintain annual revenue growth guidance of 20% to 25% year-on-year.

## Risks & Concerns

- Higher raw material costs (specifically critical raw materials like whey) impacting gross margins.
- Working capital impact resulting from inventory buildup, leading to a negative operating cash flow of INR8.6 crores for the half year.

## Segment Performance

- **Branded Segment:** Showed strong growth supported by healthy demand, field force expansion, and clinical nutrition portfolio; PentaSure accounts for over 95% of branded sales.
- **Premix Business:** Saw higher revenue growth year-on-year driven by strong order book and expanding geographic reach.
- **ESG / Tender Business:** Experienced temporary lumpiness/slowdown in Q2 due to awaiting letters of credit (LCs) from customers, with a total order book of about INR100 crores (INR75 crores expected to be shipped in H2).

## Analyst Q&A Highlights

**Q:** Despite 27% revenue growth, why did PAT decline slightly in H1?

**A:** Management cited the gross margin impact from rising raw material prices (such as whey), increased investments in people/field force leading to higher salary and admin expenses, and a one-time exceptional impact of plant modification.

---

**Q:** What are the primary drivers for inventory buildup in H1?

**A:** Inventory rose from INR84 crores to INR136 crores due to strategic procurement of key raw materials ahead of expected price hikes and preparation for confirmed H2 orders and delayed LCs. Inventory is expected to normalize by March 2027.

---

**Q:** What is the status of the Nashik facility reconstruction and associated capex?

**A:** Reconstruction work has been initiated and is being tracked regularly. Total capex for the facility modification is planned around INR25 crores, majorly funded by a bank loan of INR20 crores.

---

**Q:** What is the order book and expected execution for the ESG segment in H2?

**A:** The total ESG order book stands close to INR100 crores, out of which roughly INR75 crores is likely to be shipped in H2.
